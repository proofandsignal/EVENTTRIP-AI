# EventTrip AI — Partner application kit v0.1

Updated: 2026-10-18. Status: **PREPARED ONLY**. No partner account, approval, API entitlement or referral identifier has been verified.

## Product description (ready to copy into an application)

EventTrip AI is an independent travel discovery project in development by Proof & Signal. We are building one responsive platform for budget city breaks, premium hotel stays, and event-led trips. The first market-validation campaign will focus on European winter and Christmas travel in 2026, with English-language discovery pages and clear affiliate disclosures.

Visitors will compare their **own trip budget estimates** across transport, accommodation, baggage, transfers and experiences. We plan to refer customers to approved booking partners for checkout; EventTrip will not process traveler payments or issue tickets. We seek authorization for accurately attributed affiliate deep links and, where available, permitted product feeds or search widgets.

Public website: **not deployed yet**.
Visitors / bookings / commissions: **no verified figures yet**.
Launch target: **2026-11-05, conditional on approvals and acceptance gates**.

## Candidate onboarding — official starting points

1. **Travelpayouts**: https://www.travelpayouts.com/ ; project creation steps: https://support.travelpayouts.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021216060-How-to-start-working-with-affiliate-programs . A created Project may get immediate access to eligible programs, with others requiring review. Inspect "My Programs" and the allowed tooling before asserting readiness.
2. **Tiqets**: https://www.tiqets.com/en/partner-program/affiliate/ . Its affiliate program has no stated minimum visitor/order threshold but is still subject to eligibility and platform approval. Commission is a share of gross transaction margin, not automatically a percentage of ticket sale price.
3. **Fever**: https://business.feverup.com/en/partner-programs/affiliate-program/ . Affiliate partners need an active digital channel. Basic affiliate links can be used after enrollment; feeds and custom integration are for eligible/approved partners, not general public API rights.

**No credentials, passwords, ID documents, payout/bank details or private affiliate IDs belong in this public repository.** Fill forms via the official provider websites using the business owner's authorized contact details. Record only non-sensitive statuses and terms evidence in the partner-access issue.

## Pre-application checklist

- [ ] Verify identity of business/applicant and preferred contact mailbox outside the public repository.
- [ ] Provide an accessible, approved preview or honest prelaunch description (without claiming live traffic).
- [ ] Select appropriate program / Project type for a travel discovery website.
- [ ] Retain the official program terms, jurisdictional restrictions, content-use rights and allowed traffic sources.
- [ ] Record approval status and permitted linking/widget/API format.
- [ ] Validate attribution, reporting, payment timing and cancellation policies before forecasting commissions.

## Validation ledger (not financial claims)

Source → approved program → allowed link → click evidence → partner-reported eligible booking → verified commission → cancellation/payment status → net result after costs.

Proof of click is **not** proof of booking. Booking is **not** proof of earned and payable commission.
